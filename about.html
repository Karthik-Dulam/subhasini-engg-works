About Subhasini Engg Works

A well-equipped machine shop in Hyderabad, manufacturing quality precision components and assemblies for India's heat transfer and industrial sector.

Who We Are

Precision Engineering from Hyderabad

Subhasini Engg Works is a Hyderabad-based precision engineering company situated at SVCIE Phase-II, Balanagar. We are supported by a well-equipped machine shop, precision instruments, and skilled manpower.

We manufacture and supply quality machined components as per your drawings, specifications, or samples at competitive prices. We supply various engineering components to several leading companies in India and internationally.

We are an approved vendor (Vendor Code: S3436) at the Savli-GIDC Unit, reinforcing our commitment to quality and traceability.
