Primary Product

Bearing Block Assemblies

For Air Fin Fan Coolers

We manufacture complete Bearing Block Assemblies used in Air Fin Fan (AFF) Coolers — critical rotating equipment used in heat exchangers, refineries, petrochemical plants, and cooling systems.

Our assemblies are manufactured with precision-machined housings, shafts, and fitted bearings — built to withstand continuous industrial operation.

We supply to leading heat transfer equipment manufacturers across Mumbai, Ahmedabad, Chennai, Hyderabad, Vadodara, Solapur, Pune, and internationally to Ajman, UAE.

Specialised Tooling

Tube Finning Machine Tooling

Applied & Extruded Type

We manufacture high-precision tooling used in applied and extruded tube finning machines. Tube finning is a critical process in manufacturing finned tubes used in heat exchangers, condensers, and cooling coils.

Our tooling is designed for accuracy, durability, and consistent performance across high-volume production runs. We supply to fin tube manufacturers in Coimbatore, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Vadodara, and Mumbai.

Tooling can be manufactured as per your existing dimensions or drawings, with minimal lead time.
